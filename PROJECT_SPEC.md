## 5. Travel Logistics & Tips
- **Vehicle Selection:** 150cc+ motorcycles for Meghalaya backroads; 4x4 or high-clearance SUVs for Sela and Bum La in winter.
- **Mandatory Gear:** Waterproof dry bags, gripping water shoes for riverbeds, and life jackets for deep karst cavern swims.
- **Permits:** Inner Line Permit (ILP) required for Arunachal Pradesh; special DC/Army permits required in Tawang for Bum La Pass.
